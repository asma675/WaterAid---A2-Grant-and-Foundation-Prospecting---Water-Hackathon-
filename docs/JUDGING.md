# Judge pitch

## One sentence

GrantCurrent lets a two-person fundraising team ask a plain-English question and receive a **source-linked, eligibility-checked, capacity-aware decision brief**, without needing to know how to prompt an AI system.

## Why it matches the challenge

- **Baseline:** finds and displays opportunities with source text, links, and dates.
- **Strong:** hard gates happen before fit, missing/uncertain facts route to REVIEW, and rankings are explainable.
- **Exceptional:** the architecture supports annual-report discovery, source-change detection, relationship consent, capacity constraints, and defensible declines.

## The three moments to demo

1. **O08 — hidden opportunity:** surfaced from an annual report even though the grants page does not show it.
2. **O11 — conflict handling:** two deadline sources disagree; GrantCurrent routes to REVIEW instead of inventing certainty.
3. **K3 — strategic “no”:** high fit and high value, but too many hours for the quarter. The tool protects staff capacity.

## Differentiator

Most grant-search demos maximize the number of results. This one optimizes **decision quality per staff hour** and makes “no” a first-class output.
