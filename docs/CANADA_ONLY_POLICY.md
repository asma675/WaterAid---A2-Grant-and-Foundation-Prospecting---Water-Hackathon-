# Canada-only prospect policy

The live prospect queue enforces two rules before a structured opportunity is returned:

1. The funder or funding program is Canadian.
2. The funded project takes place in Canada.

External AI tools can suggest candidates, but suggestions are displayed only in the **AI discovery queue**. They are not treated as verified prospects until a Canadian public source confirms jurisdiction and program details.

The curated source registry intentionally prioritizes Canadian federal, provincial, municipal, corporate-foundation and independent-fund sources.
