# Canadian-funder-only policy

GrantCurrent does **not** mean projects in Canada only.

The prospect queue enforces:

1. **Funding source jurisdiction: Canada only.**
2. **Project geography: at least one active target country configured in Scope & settings.**

A user may add Canada as a project country if the organization later wants that scope, but it is not included automatically in the international WaterAid baseline.

External AI suggestions remain unverified research leads until Canadian jurisdiction, project-country relevance and first-party evidence are confirmed.
