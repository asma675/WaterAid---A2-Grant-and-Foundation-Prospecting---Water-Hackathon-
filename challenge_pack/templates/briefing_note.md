# Briefing note template

> SYNTHETIC - FICTIONAL ORGANIZATION FOR THE WATER CHALLENGE - NOT WATERAID DATA

One page a fundraiser can act on. Every fact carries its source and retrieved date. Inferred dates are labelled **Inferred** and never presented as fact.

## Header
_Funder, opportunity title, recommendation (PURSUE / QUEUE / CULTIVATE / REVIEW / MONITOR / DECLINE), fit score, confidence (High/Medium/Low)_



## Why it fits
_Up to 3 bullets, each tied to a rubric criterion (C1-C7) and a theme or country code_



## Eligibility check
_Table: gate, result (Pass/Fail/Uncertain), evidence quote, source URL, retrieved date_



## Key dates
_Each date with status: Confirmed (quote + source) or Inferred (never presented as fact)_



## Money and effort
_Grant range, likely ask, application type, fundraising + program hours, expected value, fits remaining hours?_



## Relationship path
_People with a link (consent = Yes only), strength, suggested ask of them_



## Risks and unknowns
_What could make this wrong; what to confirm with the funder_



## Next step
_One action, owner, due date_



## Sources
_Every source used, with retrieved date_

# Worked example: test case O08 (Ostrander Foundation)

**Header.** Ostrander Foundation: Global Health Systems envelope (LOI). Recommendation: PURSUE. Fit 4.0. Confidence: Medium (program not yet on funder website).

**Why it fits.** C1: WASH in healthcare facilities is named in the envelope (WASH-HCF, core). C2: sub-Saharan Africa includes Malawi, Mozambique, Ethiopia. C7: multi-year grants of $150k-$600k.

**Eligibility check.** G1 Pass: 'registered Canadian charities' (annual report p.14, retrieved 2026-10-11). G2 Pass: sub-Saharan Africa. G3 Pass: health systems incl. WASH. G4-G8 Pass. Note: grants page (retrieved 2026-10-11) does not list this program.

**Key dates.** LOI window Nov 1 - Dec 12, 2026: Confirmed (annual report p.14). Full-proposal date: unknown.

**Money and effort.** Likely ask $375k over 3 years. LOI 8 hrs now; if invited, full proposal ~60 hrs + 20 program hrs in Q4. Expected value about $110k (30% chance of award x $375k, less expected effort). Fits remaining Q3 hours: Yes.

**Relationship path.** R09 (board director) met Ostrander's CEO in 2025; strength 1; consent Yes. Ask: a short note to the CEO when the LOI goes in.

**Risks and unknowns.** Program absent from website: confirm with Ostrander that LOIs are open. No grant history in international health.

**Next step.** Officer emails Ostrander to confirm the LOI window by Oct 30; LOI drafted by Nov 20.

**Sources.** ostranderfdn.example/reports/annual-2025.pdf (2026-10-11); ostranderfdn.example/grants (2026-10-11)
