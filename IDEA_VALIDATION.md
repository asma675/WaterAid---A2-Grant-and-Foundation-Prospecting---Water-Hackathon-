# GrantCurrent v0.7 Classic — Hackathon Validation

## Core problem

WaterAid does not need another generic chatbot. The product problem is to help a small fundraising team find **Canadian sources of funding for WASH work in WaterAid countries**, understand which opportunities are actually eligible and strategically useful, and protect scarce staff hours.

## What v0.7 Classic demonstrates

1. **Evidence-backed prospecting** rather than unsupported AI answers.
2. **G1–G8 hard gates before fit** for verified public opportunities.
3. **C1–C7 fit reasoning**, source freshness and explicit unknowns.
4. **Historical fundraising intelligence** from the challenge's 104-record synthetic pipeline without pretending it is WaterAid data.
5. **Effort economics**: awarded CAD/hour, lost effort, application-type efficiency and outlier sensitivity.
6. **Human decision control**: recommendations are logged and can be overridden with reasons.
7. **Project funding planning**: budget gaps, linked opportunities and staff-hour scenarios.
8. **Grounded strategy support** through FundScope Advisor.
9. **Configurable country scope** with persistent add/remove/deactivate controls.
10. **Optional TRL** for technology-oriented projects while keeping funding stage and technology maturity separate.

## Why this is stronger than a simple grant database

A conventional database helps users find funders. GrantCurrent adds an explicit decision layer:

> source evidence → eligibility → fit → relationship → historical context → effort/capacity → human decision

The product also exposes when it does **not** know enough to recommend pursuit.

## Important limits

- Historical L1 data is fictional challenge data.
- Current public-funder records still require re-verification before submission.
- User-entered 0–100 scores are transparent prioritization heuristics, not calibrated win probabilities.
- Demo role selection is not enterprise authentication.
- TRL is a technology-maturity measure, not grant readiness or likelihood of award.
