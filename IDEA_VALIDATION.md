# Idea validation — GrantCurrent v0.3

## Problem

Water-sector fundraisers do not only need more search results. They need a fast way to find **Canadian donor/funding potential for Canadian water problems**, understand whether the funder has actually supported similar work, verify eligibility and decide what deserves scarce proposal time.

## Product thesis

**GrantCurrent helps you find Canadian water funding and decide which funding is worth pursuing.**

## Why this direction is stronger

- Familiar grant-database interaction: keyword search, filters, gift/award history and funder profiles.
- Clear weekly operating view instead of an AI-chat-first interface.
- Canada-only jurisdiction guardrail reduces irrelevant international results.
- Public award history gives behavioural evidence, not just funder marketing language.
- Source conflicts route to REVIEW rather than hallucination.
- Human decision log creates an auditable learning loop.
- Optional multi-provider AI expands discovery without becoming the source of truth.

## Main remaining gap versus a mature commercial database

Data depth. GrantCurrent ships a focused Canadian-water corpus, not decades of proprietary curated grant records. The scalable path is scheduled ingestion from public federal/provincial award datasets, funder recipient pages and approved organization-specific sources, followed by human QA.
